Executive assessment

This is a close technical match to the established RF-validation / test-automation track.

Strongest overlap:

  • RF validation and test planning.
  • Automated RF measurement and ATE development.
  • Spectrum analysers, signal generators, VNAs, scopes and related lab equipment.
  • SCPI/GPIB instrument control and scripting.
  • Receiver sensitivity, BER/BLER and RF performance validation.
  • Production and validation data handling/reporting.
  • Firmware/DUT interaction through debug consoles, register access and MCU interfaces.
  • SPI/I2C/UART familiarity and embedded-hardware awareness.
