Why interesting

Direct mission-critical radio relevance rather than generic RF adjacency. Rohill develops and delivers TETRA infrastructure, base-station systems, gateways, network-management applications, dispatch/logging solutions and associated test/commissioning services. Current first-party material describes in-house hardware and software used across public safety, transport, airports, seaports, utilities, industry and defence. This maps closely to prior professional-mobile-radio validation, integration and system-level engineering experience.

Working-style fit

Rohill is a privately held Dutch telecom company headquartered in Hoogeveen with a relatively small engineering organisation. Its current careers material describes an open, multicultural and interactive environment and explicitly invites direct/open applications. Current and recent technical roles show engineers working across configuration, production, test, validation, release processes, fault diagnosis and customer/system delivery rather than only narrow clean-sheet design.

Contractor evidence

Independent contractor / PSC engagement is not currently verified. However, a 2026 Test Developer vacancy explicitly states that Rohill products are developed in-house and partly in collaboration with external subcontractors. That is useful evidence that external engineering contributors are part of the delivery model, but it is not enough to claim a current freelance or contract-engineer hiring route.

Current route

Direct speculative outreach is credible. The strongest positioning is hands-on TETRA/LMR product engineering: RF/system validation, test automation, integration, commissioning, fault isolation, production support and performance verification. A direct question about project-based or contract support is justified because Rohill publicly references development collaboration with subcontractors and currently invites open applications.

Caveats

  • No current independent-contractor vacancy or outside-IR35 equivalent has been verified.
  • Hoogeveen is the primary engineering location, so travel/on-site expectations need checking.
  • Rohill spans hardware, software and network infrastructure; individual roles may be more software/network-heavy than the preferred hardware/RF balance.
  • TETRA domain relevance is direct, but specific P25 experience should be presented as adjacent mission-critical-radio expertise rather than protocol equivalence.
