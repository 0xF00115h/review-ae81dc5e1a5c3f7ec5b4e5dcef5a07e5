Why interesting

Current UK RF contract-market result set focused on roles with practical delivery pressure: RF bring-up, validation, hardware-in-loop, integration, EMC/debug, production support, component obsolescence, manufacturing support and existing-product troubleshooting.

This entry is intentionally consolidated to avoid Companies-page clutter. Vacancy facts are kept separate from employer attribution. Attribution grades follow the existing rule:

  • DIRECT / VERIFIED — employer identity established by first-party material or employer-controlled contingent route.
  • HIGH-CONFIDENCE INFERENCE — anonymous client, but multiple unusually specific indicators align with one company.
  • POSSIBLE / INVESTIGATIVE — some indicators align, but not enough to treat employer identity as fact.

Working-style fit

These are stronger than generic RF design jobs because the role language includes real hardware ownership: TX/RX circuits, RF modules, lab instrumentation, live integration, VNA/spectrum-analyser work, validation, debug, manufacturing/in-service support and corrective action.
