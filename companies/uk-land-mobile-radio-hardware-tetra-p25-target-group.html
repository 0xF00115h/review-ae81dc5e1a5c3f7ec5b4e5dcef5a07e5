Why interesting

Consolidated target group for UK organisations with meaningful involvement in professional / mission-critical land-mobile-radio hardware, particularly TETRA and P25. This entry deliberately groups secondary targets to avoid creating a separate Companies dossier for every organisation.

### Simoco Wireless Solutions — Derby Strongest additional UK hardware target in this group. Simoco supplies P25 portable and mobile radios, base stations, repeaters and deployable radio infrastructure. Relevant areas include radio-system integration, RF/infrastructure validation, commissioning, fault diagnosis, product support and performance testing.

Current first-party activity signal: on 3 September 2026 Simoco announced a partnership with Antevia Networks to integrate a rapidly deployable private-5G platform into Simoco's INEOS Grenadier mission-critical communications vehicle, with demonstrations planned through 2026 and beyond. Separately, Simoco announced in July 2026 that Thorcom had been fully integrated into the global Simoco business as part of continued investment intended to increase scale, innovation and growth. These are not contractor-hiring evidence and the private-5G work is adjacent to rather than equivalent to P25/TETRA, but together they strengthen the evidence that Simoco is actively integrating and extending mission-critical communications platforms rather than merely maintaining a legacy LMR catalogue.

Website: https://simoco.com/

### EARS plc — UK Smaller practical radio-engineering organisation rather than a radio OEM. Particularly interesting because its work includes systems engineering, bench work, radio configuration, commissioning, repair, installation and maintenance across professional-radio platforms including Tait, Simoco and Motorola equipment. Potentially attractive for broad hands-on problem ownership.

Current first-party hiring signal: EARS currently advertises a permanent Systems & Bench Engineer in London at £40,000+ and says the role joins an expanding engineering team. The work explicitly covers VHF/UHF professional radio, Motorola MOTOTRBO systems, RF infrastructure, component-level fault diagnosis and repair, workshop testing, commissioning, field fault-finding, system optimisation and final acceptance testing. This is evidence of current engineering demand and unusually strong hands-on lane fit, but it is permanent employment rather than independent-contractor evidence.

Website: https://earsplc.com/

### Audiolink Ltd — London Current secondary hiring evidence reports two full-time Senior Radio Engineer openings in NW London covering design, installation, configuration and maintenance of professional radio systems, technical troubleshooting, system-performance evaluation and network integration. Audiolink's first-party site independently confirms a long-established UK two-way-radio business with in-house engineers providing hire, sales, installation, service and repair across Motorola and other professional-radio platforms. The role evidence is a strong hands-on lane fit, but the vacancy itself has not been located on a first-party Audiolink careers page and independent-contractor engagement is not established.

Website: https://www.audiolink.co.uk/

### DCRS — St Ives, Cambridgeshire Current first-party hiring evidence adds DCRS as a strong practical radio-engineering target. Its live vacancies page lists a Radio Communication Engineer (Bench and Field Maintenance/Installation) role for an experienced engineer working on digital radio communications equipment. The role includes bench repair and fault rectification, installation, test and commissioning, on-site fault diagnosis, remote technical support and work with major radio manufacturers including Motorola Solutions and Hytera. The vacancy is permanent employment rather than independent-contractor evidence, and the published role is DMR-focused rather than specifically P25/TETRA, so it is adjacent professional-LMR evidence rather than proof of P25/TETRA engineering ownership.

Website: https://www.dcrs.co.uk/

### Tait Communications UK Major P25 / DMR professional-radio manufacturer with genuine portable, mobile and base-station/repeater hardware. Core product development is substantially centred outside the UK, particularly New Zealand, so the UK opportunity should be investigated for systems, integration, validation, support and project engineering rather than assuming local RF product design.

Website: https://www.taitcommunications.com/

### Motorola Solutions / Airwave UK Large incumbent mission-critical communications organisation with extensive TETRA infrastructure, subscriber hardware and the Airwave network ecosystem. Technically relevant but substantially more corporate than the preferred small/pragmatic-company profile. Worth monitoring for specialist hardware, integration, validation, obsolescence and product-support opportunities.

Website: https://www.motorolasolutions.com/

Working-style fit

The common attraction is practical land-mobile-radio engineering rather than protocol/software work alone: radio hardware, RF infrastructure, validation, test, commissioning, integration, production support, fault isolation and maintenance of deployed products.

Fit varies substantially by organisation:

  • Simoco is the strongest direct product/infrastructure target in this group.
  • EARS may offer the broadest practical bench/system ownership despite not being an OEM.
  • Audiolink is a strong service/integration target with current secondary evidence of senior radio-engineering demand, though the hiring signal is not yet first-party verified.
  • DCRS has a current first-party bench/field radio-engineering vacancy with direct repair, commissioning and fault-finding ownership, though the advertised work is DMR-focused rather than specifically P25/TETRA.
  • Tait has strong hardware relevance but UK ownership of RF design needs verification.
  • Motorola/Airwave has deep domain relevance but a much larger and more formal organisation.

Contractor evidence

No single contractor model applies across this group and no current independent-contractor route is asserted here.

Treat contractor compatibility separately for each organisation:

  • Simoco: investigate direct project/contract engineering routes.
  • EARS: small engineering-services structure makes project-based engagement worth asking about, but not verified.
  • Audiolink: current hiring evidence is full-time employment; project/contract engagement is unverified.
  • DCRS: current engineering vacancy is permanent employment; independent-contractor engagement is unverified.
  • Tait UK: contractor route unverified.
  • Motorola Solutions/Airwave: contractors are plausible in the wider programme ecosystem, but a suitable current hardware/RF engagement has not been verified.

Current route

Use this as a research/outreach queue rather than a single application target.

Priority: 1. Simoco — investigate UK engineering ownership and project/contract needs. 2. EARS — investigate bench, systems, commissioning and problem-resolution work. 3. DCRS — investigate whether current bench/field radio-engineering demand can translate into project or contract engagement. 4. Audiolink — verify the current Senior Radio Engineer hiring signal directly and ask whether project/contract radio-engineering engagement is possible. 5. Tait UK — establish what engineering responsibility exists locally. 6. Motorola Solutions/Airwave — monitor selectively for practical RF/hardware/integration work.

Position around hands-on professional-radio engineering: RF/product validation, test automation, integration, fault isolation, commissioning, production support, obsolescence/component substitution and solving difficult existing-product problems.

Caveats

  • This is intentionally a consolidated sector entry rather than one legal entity.
  • Do not infer P25 and TETRA protocol equivalence; they are separate standards within the same mission-critical LMR domain.
  • Presence of a company's hardware in the UK does not prove that RF hardware development occurs in the UK.
  • Contractor engagement must be verified individually.
  • Audiolink's current Senior Radio Engineer signal is supported by multiple secondary listings plus first-party company/service evidence, but the vacancy itself is not yet first-party verified.
  • DCRS's current role is first-party verified but is DMR-focused and permanent; do not promote it as P25/TETRA or independent-contractor evidence.
  • Existing individually curated companies such as Sepura and Etherstack remain separate and should not be replaced by this entry.
