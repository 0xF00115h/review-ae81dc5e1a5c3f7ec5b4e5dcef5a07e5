Why interesting

This lane targets the physical layer of IoT rather than cloud/platform work: sensors, gateways, wearables, telemetry, BLE/LoRa/cellular/Wi-Fi products, embedded control, low-power hardware, device bring-up, validation and field deployment. It is attractive because the work often sits exactly at electronics/firmware/RF/test boundaries and small product teams frequently need one engineer to make third-party hardware, sensors and communications behave as a complete system.

The highest-value signal is not the IoT label itself. It is ownership of real connected hardware from prototype through validation, integration and production/field use. Current research finds several named employers plus multiple anonymous contract adverts proving that this niche contains genuine temporary engineering demand.

Working-style fit

FLOWBIO — strongest current named contract hit. A current London contract for a Senior / Mid-Level Electrical / Embedded Systems Engineer covers C/C++, analogue front ends, BLE, data streaming, power management, PCB build/test and QA tooling around a real wearable sweat-sensing product. The engineer works across Engineering, R&D and Software and helps shape both hardware and firmware. This is a very strong literal physical-IoT match: sensor electronics, low-power embedded, wireless, measurement and production-facing tooling in a startup environment.

Arribada Initiative — exceptionally strong connected-device work shape; non-permanent route is fixed-term. First-party role material describes embedded hardware for remote wildlife/environmental monitoring using GPS, LoRa, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular and satellite links, with low-power design, PCB work, firmware interaction, fault finding, validation and field debugging. The current/specific role page describes a 24-month fixed-term contract; a senior firmware/systems role is similarly fixed-term. Arribada explicitly expects project ownership and client-facing field problem solving. This is one of the clearest examples of physical IoT where electronics, firmware, RF/connectivity, test and harsh-environment deployment all meet.

CSignum — fresh connected-hardware scale-up signal; current non-permanent route is fixed-term production/test employment. First-party careers material describes its Bathgate team building Internet of Underwater Things hardware around patented electromagnetic-field communications. CSignum says it is preparing for a ramp in demand and is hiring hands-on Production Technicians on a fixed-term basis to approximately Q1 2027 for electronics assembly, functional/performance test, fault diagnosis, quality records and root-cause support while it transitions assembly/test activity to a contract-manufacturing partner. A separate current Embedded Software Engineer role is permanent and includes IoT systems, hardware/software integration, regression and design-verification testing, schematics and bench instruments. This is strong evidence of active connected-product delivery pressure and cross-discipline hardware/test work, but not evidence of PSC/Outside-IR35 engineering engagement.

HealthX — historical/low-confidence route, strong product shape. HealthX is developing its own cardiovascular-monitoring wearable and publicly shows a sensor-layout prototype. A reposted Wellfound listing sought an experienced PCB/microelectronics engineer for IoT/Bluetooth/wearable work on a part-time/contractor basis initially, with potential conversion to full time. The route should be reverified because the listing is old/reposted, but it is useful evidence that the company has previously considered contractor-style electronics input.

Carv — strong employer resonance, no contractor route found. Current first-party hiring is now for a Senior Embedded Systems Engineer working across connected sensor firmware, board bring-up, lab electronics debugging, BLE, next-generation sensing hardware, production firmware, automated test equipment/test jigs, manufacturing yield and field diagnostics. This remains an unusually good connected-product work pattern, but no external-engineering route is stated in the current first-party opening.

MICHELIN Connected Fleet — strong connected-hardware integration, weaker structural fit. Current hardware work covers telematics, embedded cameras, CAN-bus vehicle data, firmware/AI integration, supplier hardware qualification and contract-manufacturer management. It is a larger and more structured environment than the ideal small-team target, and the current role is permanent, but it demonstrates a useful employer class: connected fleet/edge-product teams where system integration, third-party hardware and validation matter more than pure schematic design.

Anonymous Basildon smart-device company — historical/reverify work-pattern signal; end client deliberately unresolved. The previously cited Hireful IoT/Systems Integration and Test/Validation/Integration role URLs now redirect as expired. The underlying work pattern remains relevant historical evidence: sourcing IoT devices and sensors, DVT/PVT testing, troubleshooting physical/electronic/data interfaces and owning integration from concept to market. Employment basis was internally inconsistent across listings, so no current contract/permanent route should be inferred. Do not infer the end client from location or product clues.

Anonymous London wearable business via Zenovo — strong Outside-IR35 market evidence. A recent London/Hybrid Outside-IR35 Hardware & Embedded Software Engineer contract is described around intelligent wearable products combining embedded systems, sensors, BLE, low-power battery design, lab debugging, test automation and manufacturing/certification. The client is not named, so retain as proof of demand rather than promote a company identity.

Anonymous IoT/lighting client via ReeVR — recent contractor-market evidence. A recruiter advertised a six-month remote Outside-IR35 contract around embedded devices and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth/LoRa/Zigbee/BLE, ideally lighting. The role is C#-weighted and the client is anonymous, but it confirms another physical-IoT contractor pocket that conventional electronics searches may miss.

Databuzz is an IT services/consulting organisation, so treat it as a possible delivery/intermediary route rather than automatically as the underlying product employer.

Current route

Prioritise this lane through search terms that describe physical-system work rather than generic IoT:

  • sensor integration, connected device, wearable, telemetry, gateway, edge hardware;
  • BLE, LoRaWAN, LTE-M, NB-IoT, cellular, GNSS, Matter, Thread when paired with hardware/embedded/test language;
  • DVT, PVT, NPI, bring-up, validation, field trial, low power, battery, production test, hardware firmware integration.

Immediate named outreach/watch priorities from this pass are FLOWBIO, Arribada, HealthX and Carv. CSignum is a fresh watch/direct-approach candidate because current scale-up and manufacturing-transition evidence establishes delivery pressure, but the verified non-permanent opening is production/test fixed-term employment rather than an engineering PSC route. MICHELIN Connected Fleet is useful employer intelligence but lower priority because of scale/permanent structure. Anonymous recruiter adverts should be used to question recruiters directly about end-client, project stage and contractor mechanism rather than guessed into canonical companies.

Caveats

  • IoT searches are noisy and dominated by cloud/software roles. Keep the physical-device requirement hard.
  • Do not equate fixed-term employment with PSC/Outside-IR35 contracting.
  • Do not infer anonymous Basildon, Zenovo or ReeVR clients from geography/product descriptions.
  • Some job-board employment-type metadata is internally inconsistent; role text and direct recruiter confirmation take precedence.
  • Connected-product teams can become highly software/cloud-centric as they scale; evaluate whether bench access and hardware ownership remain real.
  • Defence/autonomous-weapons connected-device roles remain excluded even when the technology looks highly aligned.
