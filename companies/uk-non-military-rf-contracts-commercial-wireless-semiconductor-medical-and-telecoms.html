Caveats

  • Hard exclusion: military/defence work, weapons, missiles, tactical systems, military radar and explicitly defence-cleared programmes are out of scope.
  • Security clearance is not automatically proof of military work in every UK role, but where the programme is defence/security or weapons-related it should be excluded rather than merely down-ranked.
  • Mission-critical alone is ambiguous: public safety, healthcare and commercial infrastructure can be mission-critical without being military. Check the end application before rejecting or accepting.
  • Anonymous commercial wireless/semiconductor clients still need end-use confirmation before outreach progresses.
  • Forefront RF is a strong employer fingerprint for the older Cambridge cellular-test contract pattern, but that specific contract client remains inferred; current named Forefront RF vacancies are separate first-party evidence and do not verify a contractor route.

  • WA Consultants currently advertises a separate Surrey Analog & Mixed Signal Verification (Rx / Tx) contract, but its work is simulation/test-bench debug rather than physical RF lab bring-up, so it is not promoted into this hands-on RF priority set.
  • A newly surfaced WA Cambridgeshire RF Systems contract explicitly includes radar, secure communications and defence-related systems, so it is excluded by this lane's hard non-military constraint.
