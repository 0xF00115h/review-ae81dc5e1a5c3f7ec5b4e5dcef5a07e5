Why interesting

Very strong technical fit around mission-critical radio products, TETRA/LTE, integrated RF products, validation, manufacturing support, obsolescence resolution and real-world debug. TETRA is also strongly adjacent to the same mission-critical land-mobile-radio domain as P25, so prior Etherstack/P25 experience is directly relevant domain continuity rather than merely generic RF similarity. This is closer to existing-product engineering and practical RF problem resolution than a narrow isolated-component design role.

Working-style fit

The available contractor adverts describe hands-on work across complete radio products rather than isolated RF blocks, including design, validation, integration, debug, component obsolescence and manufacturing-engineering support. Sepura itself describes a Cambridge-based mission-critical TETRA/LTE product business supplying public-safety and other critical users worldwide. The environment is more structured than a micro-company, but the engineering work can still be strongly practical and product-centred.

Strong current outside-IR35 contractor signal, with an attribution caveat. One advert describes the client as a global/market leader in mission-critical communications serving police, paramedics, sea rescue, airports, mining, utilities and hazardous environments. However, the recruiter adverts do not explicitly name Sepura, so the client attribution remains high-confidence inference rather than verified fact.

Treat Sepura as an active contractor target.
