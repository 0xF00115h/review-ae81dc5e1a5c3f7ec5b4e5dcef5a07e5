Why interesting

Very strong practical fit for broad electronics/RF/product-support engineering. Current roles span analogue and digital electronics, Microwave RF support, prototyping, design verification, production support, test facilities, radar proving, fault finding, automated test and existing-product improvement. The Production Electronics Engineer role explicitly asks for breadth rather than narrow specialism and for someone willing to get into the detail while working across small project teams.

Working-style fit

First-party careers material currently emphasises autonomy and trust. Role material describes Blighter as an SME able to adapt designs quickly, and asks engineers to manage their own work, integrate into small project teams and take substantial subsystem/product-lifecycle ownership. This is a stronger direct working-style signal than size alone.

Contractor evidence

Current non-permanent engineering route verified, but PSC/Ltd-company contracting is not. Blighter currently advertises both Electronics Engineer (2 Year Contract) and Senior Electronics Engineer (2 Year Contract), explicitly classified as fixed-term contracts. The senior role includes ownership from prototyping and circuit design through design verification and production support. This demonstrates willingness to bring experienced electronics engineers in on a defined non-permanent term, but it must not be represented as evidence of outside-IR35, freelance or personal-service-company engagement.

Current route

Direct approach is credible. The strongest question is whether Blighter would consider an experienced electronics/RF engineer on a project or consultancy contract instead of the advertised two-year fixed-term employment route. Their careers page also explicitly invites speculative CVs when the desired role is not listed.

Caveats

  • Current engineering contracts are fixed-term employment, not confirmed independent-contractor engagements.
  • Defence, border-security and counter-drone work is central to the business; project exposure and clearance implications need checking.
  • Some current electronics roles include new circuit design, so role-by-role balance between clean-sheet design and integration/problem-resolution should be assessed.
  • Hybrid roles generally require regular presence at Great Chesterford.

Private notes

Keep the evidence categories separate: strong technical and working-style fit; active non-permanent engineering hiring; independent-contractor compatibility still unverified. A direct project/contract proposal is justified, but should be framed as a question rather than an assumed hiring model.
